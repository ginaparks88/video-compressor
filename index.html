How shrinking works
  • It works out the best quality that fits your limit from the video's length.
  • When the limit is tight it makes the picture smaller (720p, 540p, 480p...) and caps it at 30 frames per second. A smaller sharp picture looks better than a blocky full-size one.
  • Your original video is never changed.
  • On iPhone the result is MP4. On Android it is MP4 on recent Chrome, otherwise WebM (Discord plays both).

      What it can convert
      • Photos to JPG, PNG or WebP, several at once, with optional resizing. iPhone HEIC photos work on iPhone; most Android browsers can't open HEIC.
      • A video to MP4 (on Android, WebM if this phone's Chrome can't make MP4), to a GIF (the first 15 seconds, no sound), or just its sound as MP3 or WAV.
      • Audio files to MP3 or WAV. Making MP3 needs an internet connection the first time.
      • Your original files are never changed.
